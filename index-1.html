Questions

When is the best time to see the Milky Way?

Clear, moonless nights from late spring to early autumn. Check the moon phase before you book.

Is there heating?

Yes. Every cabin has a wood stove, and we leave the first night of wood stacked.

Can I bring a telescope?

Please do. Spruce has a shelf made for one, and the lodge lends a basic pair of binoculars.

What if it is cloudy?

You can move your dates once, free of charge, up to 48 hours before check in.
